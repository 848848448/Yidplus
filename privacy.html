Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YID PLUS ("YID PLUS", "we", "us", or "our"), operating the website and app at yidplus.com (the "Service"), collects, uses, and protects your information. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

This document is a general template and is not legal advice. Before relying on it, have it reviewed by a qualified attorney in your jurisdiction. Items marked [in brackets] should be completed by the operator.

1. Who we are

The Service is operated by [legal operator / company name] ("the Operator"). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@yidplus.com or through the in-app Help & Support chat.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details — your email address, nickname/username, and password (passwords are stored only as a salted cryptographic hash; we never store your plain password).
  • Profile information — anything you add to your profile, such as a bio, location, birthday, website, social links, and profile or banner photos.
  • Content you create — messages, group and channel posts, comments, statuses, shorts, voice notes, polls, stickers, and any media (images, video, audio, files) you upload.
  • Support communications — messages and screenshots you send us through Help & Support.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage & log data — sign-in times, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, browser/device type, and actions taken in the app, used for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.
  • Session data — a session token stored on your device to keep you signed in.
  • Push subscription — if you enable notifications, the technical subscription needed to deliver them.

Information from third parties

  • Google Sign-In — if you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile information (name, email, and profile picture) as permitted by your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Telegram — if you use any Telegram bridge or bot feature, limited information is exchanged with Telegram to deliver those messages.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account and provide the Service's features.
  • To deliver, sync, and display your messages, posts, and media.
  • To send you service email (such as email verification and password resets) and, if enabled, notifications and announcements.
  • To keep the Service secure — detecting spam, abuse, fraud, and violations of our Terms.
  • To provide moderation and respond to reports and support requests.
  • To improve and maintain the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. How your content is shared

  • With other users — content you send in a chat, group, or channel is visible to the members of that conversation; content you post publicly (e.g., public posts, statuses, or your profile) is visible according to the privacy settings you choose.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising that pays to target you based on your private messages.
  • Service providers — we share limited data with vendors who help us run the Service (see Section 5).
  • Legal — we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or the Operator.

5. Service providers we rely on

  • Cloudflare — hosting, database (D1), file storage (R2), and network security.
  • Google — optional sign-in.
  • Resend — sending service email.
  • Telegram — optional bridge/bot messaging, where you use it.

These providers process data on our behalf and are expected to protect it. Their own handling of data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete content or your account, we remove or de-identify the associated data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain certain records for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Backups may persist for a limited time before being overwritten.

7. Your choices & rights

  • Access & correction — you can view and edit your profile and content in the app.
  • Deletion — you can delete your content and request account deletion at support@yidplus.com.
  • Notifications — you can turn push and email notifications on or off.
  • Privacy settings — you control who can see your profile, follow you, and message you.

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or similar laws), you may have additional rights such as data portability or the right to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including hashed passwords (salted PBKDF2), encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls, and rate limiting. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly to any issues.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children under [minimum age, e.g. 13]. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it. We take child safety seriously and prohibit any content or conduct that sexualizes or endangers minors.

10. International users

The Service is operated using infrastructure that may process and store data in various countries. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer and processing of your information in locations that may have different data-protection laws than your own.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email support@yidplus.com or use the in-app Help & Support chat.
