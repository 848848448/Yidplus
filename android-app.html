Install

  1. Download the APK, then open it. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. Turn that permission off again after installing.
  2. Use an updated compatible browser such as Chrome. Sign in to your YID PLUS account.
  3. For notifications, enable them in YID PLUS Settings and allow the browser/app notification permission.

Before you install

If you have the old debug app, Android may require uninstalling it first because this release has a different signing key. Your server account and content remain; local preferences may differ.

Downloads follow the site's format and saving restrictions. The app needs an internet connection for account content.

Build, lint and signature checks passed. Actual phone launch, push delivery and MP3 downloads still need a device test.
